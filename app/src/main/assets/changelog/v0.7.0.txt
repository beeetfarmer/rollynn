Added
• Immersive lyrics: while music plays, the player controls fade away after a few seconds so the lyrics fill the screen. Tap anywhere to bring the controls back.
• Setting for immersive lyrics: "Hide controls behind lyrics" sets how many seconds to wait (0–10, default 4). Set it to 0 to keep the controls showing.
• Animated lyrics background made from the album art's colours.
• Background vocals from TTML lyrics now show under their line, alongside romanization and translations.
• ListenBrainz scrobble counts for the track you're playing.
• "What's new" dialog after an update.
• Backups now include pinned playlists, playlist folders, the home screen layout and ListenBrainz settings.
• Restore server logins from a backup on a fresh install.

Changed
• New app icon: blue for the normal app, purple for degoogled.
• One tap on the mini player opens Now Playing.
• Last.fm keys refused by Last.fm (it requires Pro from 1 December 2026) now show up in settings instead of failing silently, and are tried again once a day.

Fixed
• The app now goes offline when a VPN is the only connection left.
• The app now tells you when a new version is out.
• Closing the player no longer flashes a solid block behind the mini player.
• The Settings tab is kept when the screen is recreated.
• The album list is kept when you go back to it.
• Album shuffle now uses the clock as its seed, so it isn't the same every time.
